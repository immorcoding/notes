# Uses wayfinder; misses the right moment for prototypes

The learner uses `/wayfinder` and calls `/prototype` by hand mid-map, often too late. Specs and tickets alone drifted from real requirements, mostly on visual questions. They didn't know wayfinder has a built-in `prototype` ticket type that is chosen while charting. Teach: decide prototype tickets at charting time, and write the preference into the map's **Notes**.

**Evidence**: self-reported on 2026-10-15.
